# Hades Discord Bot 2.2.1

## Food-related conversation
- Dedicated food-chat intent covers hunger, cravings, food preferences, favorite dishes, reactions to meals, and deciding what to eat.
- Hades responds as a character and may share an in-character preference without claiming it is official canon.
- Recipes, ingredient lists, and cooking instructions remain outside the bot's intended scope.

## Existing behavior retained
- Hades's dynamic persona and contextual fan-service remain generated by Gemini.
- Aether Gazer scope remains strict.
- Automatic image/GIF cooldown behavior and expired-memory cleanup remain in place.
- The GitHub Actions smoke and regression-test suite verifies the changes.
