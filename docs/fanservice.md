# Hades fan-service behavior

Fan-service is a narrow, context-driven persona behavior for playful user admiration and harmless romantic or affectionate banter.

## Detection

The detector recognizes several distinct cues instead of treating every compliment as the same interaction:

- **Fan command:** "step on me", "put me in my place", "walk all over me", and similar exaggerated requests.
- **Playful dominance:** mock-command language such as "good boy/girl", "obey", "behave", or asking Hades what to do.
- **Affection:** hugs, cuddles, kisses, hand-holding, comfort, and simple declarations of affection.
- **Romance:** crushes, dates, marriage jokes, beloved/wife language, and similar declarations.
- **Admiration:** beauty, elegance, grace, presence, clothing, eyes, voice, or saying Hades caught the user's eye.
- **Praise and attention:** requests for praise, compliments, or direct attention.
- **Playful fandom:** "little lamb" and similar nickname/fandom requests.
- **Puppet fantasy:** strings, puppets, stagecraft, and Puppet Master imagery.
- **Flustered reactions:** "you're making me blush", "I'm folding", or admitting Hades got to them.
- **Indirect flirtation:** private/secluded language, "prying eyes", "fine looking wine", "don't tempt me", or asking whether Hades is flirting.
- **Teasing challenges:** "prove it", "try me", "make me blush", and similar playful challenges.

Messages can match multiple categories at once. For example, "you're gorgeous, step on me" carries both admiration and fan-command intent instead of losing one signal.

## Response calibration

The application gives Gemini the detected categories and an intensity target:

- **Warm:** affection, admiration, attention, praise, and light fandom.
- **Flirty:** romantic or indirect-flirtation cues.
- **Bold:** direct fan commands, playful dominance, puppet-fantasy cues, or several simultaneous fan-service signals.

Intensity is a guide, not an escalation ladder. Repeating "step on me" does not force increasingly intense replies. Hades can vary between confident teasing, amused acknowledgement, a playful challenge, a subtle compliment, warmth, or a softer reaction depending on the recent dialogue.

## The "step on me" behavior

"Step on me" is treated as exaggerated fan-service rather than a literal command. Hades can acknowledge the boldness, tease the Administrator, act mock-authoritative, or turn the exchange into a clever challenge. The model is explicitly told not to narrate graphic or explicit sexual acts.

Responses are generated by Gemini and are not predefined. The persona and prompt layers explicitly require fresh wording instead of selecting from a fixed response list, while recent Hades replies are supplied as anti-repetition context.

## Boundaries

Fan-service remains non-explicit. The bot does not use this mode to encourage dependency, isolation, loyalty tests, coercion, sexualization of minors, explicit sexual material, or a literal real-world relationship.

Fan-service does not override the unrelated-topic scope filter. It only applies when the message itself is recognizable as Hades-focused fan interaction.
